# Jotwisp logo concept v1

Generated using the built-in image-generation tool and approved by the user. The source remains an unchanged opaque presentation image. `scripts/make-icon.swift` exports its approved tile into the app’s transparent `.icns` representations; `scripts/build.sh` packages it into Jotwisp. The sidebar uses the same bundled app icon.

## Initial generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: original macOS app icon and primary logo symbol for Jotwisp, a fast, lightweight native text scratchpad that quietly saves your thoughts.
Primary request: create one exceptionally polished, aesthetic, Apple-like in craftsmanship but original in identity app icon. Quiet, minimal, tactile, elegant. Not a brand board or mockup.
Subject: a single sculptural folded-paper ribbon forming a beautifully balanced lowercase j / rising wisp silhouette. One confident curled stroke, gently tapered at its upper tip, with a softly hooked bottom; a tiny detached rounded paper-shaped dot only if it helps the j read clearly. The symbol should suggest both a fleeting thought and a little piece of paper kept safely. Avoid a generic flame silhouette.
Style: precision-crafted contemporary macOS icon, restrained soft dimensionality, rounded edges and subtle studio illumination, crisp simple silhouette recognizable at small sizes. Center the original mark on a warm-white porcelain squircle tile with a very delicate beveled edge. Use the existing app's restrained periwinkle-indigo accent for the symbol, with subtle tonal shading, not rainbow gradients. The mark must be visually bold enough to read in the Dock.
Composition: square 1024x1024 asset, straight-on orthographic view, centered icon tile occupying approximately 86% of the canvas, generous balanced inset around the symbol. Actual transparent background outside the tile, including the corners. No environmental scene.
Constraints: one icon only, no text, no lettering other than the abstract j shape, no slogan, no watermark, no Apple logo, no existing app logos, no pencil, no notebook clipart, no sparkles, no busy lines, no dramatic cast shadow, no glass glare, no presentation board. Prioritize silhouette, optical balance, and understated finish.

## Final presentation edit prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit
Create the final polished presentation of this Jotwisp logo.
Preserve exactly the periwinkle folded-paper lowercase j symbol and its soft dimensional shading, and the warm white rounded-square tile.
Replace ALL transparency and ALL pixels outside the tile with a perfectly solid opaque very pale cool gray background (#EDEEF2), uniform edge to edge. The final image must be fully opaque. Remove any grainy white flecks and rough remnants along the tile boundary: it must be clean and smoothly rounded. No cutout, no alpha transparency, no checkerboard, no speckled edge.
One centered straight-on icon, generous clean margins, no words or other elements. Keep the original proportions and visual identity. This is an aesthetic logo concept presentation, not a transparency export.
