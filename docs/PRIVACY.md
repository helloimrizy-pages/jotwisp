# Jotwisp Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

Jotwisp is a local text scratchpad. No account is required. The app does not
send your drafts, clipboard contents, searches, or usage information to its
developer. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs.

## Your text stays on your Mac

Draft text and workspace settings are stored locally. Search runs on your Mac.
Jotwisp does not provide cloud sync or upload text to an AI service. You can
paste text from an AI tool, but Jotwisp does not generate or process it remotely.
Your operating system, backup software, or a folder you explicitly export to
may have separate synchronization or backup behavior.

## Clipboard and keyboard shortcuts

Jotwisp reads clipboard text only when you invoke Paste or New Draft from
Clipboard, including through the capture shortcut. It does not keep a clipboard
history or poll the clipboard in the background.

The Mac App Store edition uses a registered modifier-and-key shortcut. It does
not request Accessibility or Input Monitoring access. The source-built edition
also supports the legacy Command+C+D chord. That edition uses Accessibility
access to recognize the chord; it does not log or save other keystrokes. You can
revoke access in System Settings. Menu-based capture remains available.

## File access and deletion

Import and export access files you select. In the Mac App Store edition,
the app uses Apple's sandbox and system file pickers.
Drafts remain on your Mac until you delete them. Trashed drafts remain
recoverable in Jotwisp until you confirm permanent deletion. Exported copies
and external backups must be managed separately. Removing the app may not
remove its stored drafts. Contact support for instructions if you need help.

Local draft files are plain text, not individually encrypted by Jotwisp.
Your Mac's login, file permissions, and optional FileVault protect access at
the operating-system level. Do not treat Jotwisp as a password manager.

## Purchases and support

Apple handles Mac App Store payments and purchase records under Apple's own
privacy policy. The developer does not receive your payment-card details.
Apple may provide store sales reports or diagnostics according to your Apple
settings; Jotwisp does not add its own analytics service.

If you email support, the developer receives the address, message, and any
attachments you choose to send, and uses them to respond to your request.
Do not send passwords or private drafts. Support emails are kept only as
needed to handle the request and related follow-up or legal obligations;
you can request deletion by email. Public GitHub issues and contributions
are handled by GitHub and are visible to others: use synthetic examples.
